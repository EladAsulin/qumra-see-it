Qumra Capital · Brand film

Same Move. Two Worlds.

A brand film for Qumra Capital · Draft 2 · approx. 75 sec

Qumra CapitalInspirationSee / HearVoice-over

Every shot is a split screen. One half is the business of growth: capital, founders, numbers, decisions. The other half is what that growth is for: the pleasure of a life well lived. A single shape or movement crosses the dividing line and joins the two worlds, so the cut feels like one continuous gesture. Smart investment on one side, the joy it makes possible on the other.

Business halfLifestyle halfThe line that joins them

Inspiration

Six split-screen films that show the language we are building on. Tap a frame to enlarge it.

01

Häagen-Dazs Love the Mix

Forsman & Bodenfors · dir. Torben Kjelstrup · 2021

A horizontal split joins two worlds into one strange, happy body: a marching band on the street above, swimmers' legs underwater below.

Why it matters: Two halves that read as one: the format itself says 'two flavours, one experience'.

Watch the film ↗
02

Carvana The Feeling

Stadium · dir. Ryan Simpson · 2021

One half shows the transaction on a tablet, the other half is already the celebration: a pool dive, a party. The dividing line moves and changes direction with the action.

Why it matters: Business on one side, the pleasure it unlocks on the other. That is the exact structure of our film.

Watch the film ↗
03

Air New Zealand Airpoints OneSmart

MercerBell · 2012

Two places, one composition: a beach, bare feet in two sands, a bridge in two countries. The lines continue across the split.

Why it matters: Matching horizon, camera height and movement so the eye reads two worlds as one frame.

Watch the film ↗
04

Cassius ft. Cat Power & Pharrell Williams Go Up (music video)

Division · dir. Alexandre Courtès · 2017

The whole clip is a split screen. Shapes and movements carry from one half to the other: a statue's arms, a rocket's plume, a cue and a splash.

Why it matters: Shape match-cuts across the line: the playful, surprising pairings we want between finance and lifestyle.

Watch the film ↗
05

Movistar Connected

Y&R Peru · Fat Free Films · 2010

Strangers are joined through split screens: one face made of two children's halves, a goalkeeper and a volleyball player in one move.

Why it matters: Partnership made visible: two people completing one gesture.

Watch the film ↗
06

Nike You Can't Stop Us

Wieden+Kennedy Portland · dir. Oscar Hudson · 2020 · Cannes Lions Film Grand Prix

90 seconds, 36 split-screen pairs chosen from about 4,000 clips. Each pair is matched in motion, colour and composition, so the centre line disappears.

Why it matters: The benchmark for precision: when the match is exact, two people in two places become one movement.

Watch the film ↗
